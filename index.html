What you're paying for

The amount is yours to decide. This is what comes with it by default.

The work itself
  • Clean, reviewable PR — small diff, linked issue, clear description
  • Tests added, CI green before merge
  • Docs, changelog and migration notes where relevant
  • I respond to review comments after you've paid
The paperwork
  • Paystack receipt emailed to you instantly
  • Printable receipt with the transaction reference
  • Reference includes your GitHub handle and PR link
  • Full refund if the PR is rejected for reasons in my control

FAQ

Everything about paying a custom amount.

Because every job is different. A one-line fix and a two-week feature shouldn't share a price list. We agree on a number in the issue, and you enter that exact amount here. If you're unsure, ask me first — I'll quote in writing before you pay.

₦100 (about $0.06) — that's Paystack's floor. There's no maximum, but for anything above ₦1,000,000 let's talk first so the settlement and receipt details are right.

You can type the amount in USD and the page converts it at the rate shown in the summary. Paystack still charges in Nigerian Naira — international cards are supported and converted automatically.

No. The amount you type is the amount charged. Paystack deducts its transaction fee from the settlement, so what lands in the subaccount is slightly less than what you paid — the same as any card payment.

Paystack only. The checkout runs inside Paystack's PCI-DSS compliant iframe; this page sends your email and the amount, never card data.

If a PR is rejected for reasons within my control, you get a full refund within 7 days of asking. If the scope grows mid-way, we re-agree the amount in writing before any extra payment.
